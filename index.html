Harshit Sharma

I build trustworthy machine learning for health: models that stay private, fair, and interpretable on messy, multimodal clinical and sensor data.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer and Information Science at Arizona State University (expected December 2027), advised by Dr. Asif Salekin in the Ubiquitous and Intelligent Sensing Lab, and a Fulton Fellow. My research covers privacy-preserving ML, explainable AI, and fairness for EHR claims, wearables and nearables (physiological signals, IMU, thermal, UWB radar), and speech. I work with clinicians in addiction medicine, speech-language pathology, and geriatrics. In Summer 2026 I was a Research Scientist Intern at Optum, UnitedHealth Group, working on language models over longitudinal EHR claims.
